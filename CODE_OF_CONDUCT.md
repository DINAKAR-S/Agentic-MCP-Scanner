# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- accepting responsibility, apologising to those affected by our mistakes, and learning
  from them
- focusing on what is best for the project rather than for ourselves

Unacceptable behaviour:

- sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- public or private harassment
- publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without
  their explicit permission

## A note specific to this project

This is a security tool, so disagreements about whether a finding is real are normal and
welcome. **Argue about the code, not the person who wrote it.** A report that a rule is
wrong is a contribution, not an attack, and it should be received that way.

If you are reporting a vulnerability in someone else's project that this tool found,
follow their disclosure policy. Do not use this repository's issue tracker to disclose a
vulnerability in third-party software.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to **dinakars2003@gmail.com**. All complaints will be
reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy and security
will be respected.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this code in good faith may face temporary or
permanent repercussions as determined by other maintainers.
